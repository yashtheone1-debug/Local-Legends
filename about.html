All About Us

The creators of this website were Yash Panchal, Ahil Patel, and Muhammad Kashif, three 7th grade boys who joined bpa and decided to interview their schools security guard and make a website to honor him. The main coder was Yash Panchal, the secondary coder was Ahil Patel, and the designer and took the part to make the presentaion was Muhammad Kashif.

Yash Panchal
